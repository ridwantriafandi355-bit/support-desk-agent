# Driftwood Supply Co. Store Policies (fictional demo store)

## Order processing
- Orders ship within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding US holidays).
- A shipping confirmation email with a tracking link is sent when the order ships.

## Shipping options and times (after dispatch)
- US Standard (USPS or UPS Ground): 3-5 business days. $6.95, free on orders of $75 or more.
- US Express (UPS 2nd Day Air): 1-2 business days. $19.00. Express orders placed before 12:00 ET on a business day ship the same day.
- Canada: 5-10 business days. $14.95.
- United Kingdom, European Union and Australia: 7-14 business days, tracked via DHL. $24.95.
- We do not ship to other countries yet.
- International orders (Canada, UK, EU, Australia) ship DDU: import duties and VAT, if any, are collected by the carrier on delivery and are not included in our prices.

## Late, lost or missing parcels
- If a parcel is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, or tracking has not updated for 7 days, we open an investigation with the carrier. If the parcel is lost, we reship it or refund it.
- If tracking says delivered but the parcel is missing: check around the property and with neighbors, and wait 24 hours (carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered early). If it still hasn't arrived, contact us and we will file a claim with the carrier.

## Cancellations and changes
- Orders can be cancelled or changed (items or shipping address) only before they ship, usually within a few hours of ordering.
- After an order ships it can't be cancelled or edited. The customer can contact the carrier with the tracking number to request a redirect, or return the items under the return policy.

## Returns
- Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging.
- Coffee can be returned only if the bag is unopened.
- Clearance items marked "Final sale" can't be returned or exchanged, unless they arrived damaged or defective.
- To start a return, go to https://returns.driftwood-supply.example and enter the order number and email address.
- US returns: we email a prepaid return label and deduct $6.95 from the refund. International returns: the customer ships the item back at their own cost to the address shown in the returns portal.
- We don't do direct exchanges. Return the item and place a new order.

## Refunds
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3 business days after we receive and inspect the return.
- Banks can take 5-10 business days to show the refund on a statement.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
- Report it within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the item (and the packaging if damaged in transit).
- We send a free replacement or a full refund including shipping. If we need the item back, we send a prepaid return label.

## Warranty
- Stoves, lanterns and grinders have a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. It doesn't cover normal wear, misuse or accidental damage.

## Safety
- If a product smells of gas, leaks, overheats or sparks, stop using it immediately and contact us.

## Pricing and discount codes
- One discount code per order. Codes must be applied at checkout and can't be added after purchase.
- One-time price adjustment: if an item you bought drops in price within 7 days of purchase, contact support to request the difference.

## Support hours
- Monday-Friday, 9:00-17:00 Eastern Time. We reply within 1 business day.
- Wholesale and partnership inquiries are handled by our partnerships team via support.
